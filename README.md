Data Analytics Internship – Project 1(OTT Streaming Content Trend Analysis)

This repository contains my submission for the OTT Streaming Content Trend Analysis Project of the Data Analytics Internship.

Dataset - Netflix Titles Dataset

Objective - The objective was to explore a streaming platform's content catalog, identify patterns in genre popularity, content ratings, release trends, content types, and regional content mix, and develop actionable content strategy recommendations.

Tools Used
1. Google Colab
2. DB Browser for SQLite
3. Notepad

OTT Content Trend Analysis
1. Used the Netflix Titles Dataset containing 8,807 records and 12 columns.
2. Inspected the dataset for missing values, duplicate records, data types, and content information.
3. Cleaned missing values in descriptive fields and converted date information for analysis.
4. Created additional analysis fields including year added, month added, duration value, and season count.
5. Analyzed the distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
6. Analyzed the number of titles added to Netflix by year.
7. Identified the Top 10 most common genres.
8. Analyzed the distribution of content ratings.
9. Identified the Top 10 countries represented in the Netflix catalog.
10. Compared Movies and TV Shows across recent release years.
11. Used SQL queries to perform core aggregations for content type, year, ratings, genres, countries, and release years.
12. Developed a Content Strategy Memo based on the findings.

Key Results
1. Total Titles Analyzed - 8,807
2. Movies - 6,131
3. TV Shows - 2,676
4. Peak Titles Added in a Year - 2,016 in 2019
5. Top Country - United States with 2,818 titles
6. 2021 Release Year - 315 TV Shows compared with 277 Movies

Key Insights
1. Netflix's content additions grew rapidly from 2016 and reached a peak in 2019 before slowing in 2020 and 2021.
2. Movies remain the larger part of the overall catalog, but TV Shows have become increasingly important in recent releases.
3. The United States has the largest representation in the catalog, while international markets such as India, the United Kingdom, Japan, and South Korea contribute significant content.

Content Strategy Recommendations
1. Focus on high-potential and strategically relevant content rather than simply increasing catalog volume.
2. Continue investing in high-quality TV Shows and serialized content.
3. Strengthen localized and international content to support different regional audiences and global growth.

Conclusion
The OTT Streaming Content Trend Analysis Project was successfully completed using Python, SQL, and Excel. The analysis identified important patterns in Netflix's content catalog, including content growth, the increasing importance of TV Shows, and the strong presence of international content. The findings were used to develop actionable recommendations for content acquisition, content quality, and international content strategy.
